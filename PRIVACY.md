# Privacy model, planned

One local user controls business data on the computer. No analytics/hosted account/remote synchronization in Fork B by default. Claude and Gmail are optional and must never be required for normal manual invoicing. Receipt extraction with Claude sends explicitly selected business document content to an external provider after informed consent. Gmail requires user authorization; sending requires approval of exact recipients, subject, body and attachment.

Real data retention, deletion, provider disclosures, customer consent, and public Google OAuth requirements are **not yet verified**. This is a design direction, not a legal-compliance certification.
